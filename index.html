Our Mission

To provide compassionate care, dignity, support and meaningful assistance to elderly and Disabled people while contributing positively to the Alexandra community.

What We Do

Home-Based Care

Our volunteers visit older persons and persons with disabilities at home to provide basic care support, monitoring, and engagement.

Social Support

We run community outreach programmes, welfare checks, and social activities to reduce isolation and improve well-being.

Advocacy

We help beneficiaries access government services, clinics, and grants, and advocate for thgeir rights and dignity.

Make a Difference Today

Join our volunteers or support our programs to help elders and persons with disabilities live with dignity.
