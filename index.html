Our Mission

To provide compassionate care, dignity, support and meaningful assistance to older persons and persons with disabilities while contributing positively to the Alexandra community.

What We Do

Home-Based Care

Our volunteers visit older persons and persons with disabilities at home to provide basic care support, monitoring and engagement.

Social Support

We support community outreach, welfare checks and social activities aimed at reducing isolation and improving well-being.

Advocacy

We help beneficiaries access relevant government services, healthcare and social grants while promoting their rights and dignity.
