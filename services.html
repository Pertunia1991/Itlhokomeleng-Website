Our Services

Support designed to promote dignity, safety, and inclusion for older persons and persons with disabilities.

Home-Based Care

Itlhokomeleng provides compassionate home-based support to older persons and persons with disabilities in the comfort of their own homes. Our services include home visits, basic personal care and daily-living assistance, wellbeing monitoring, companionship, and identifying needs that may require professional intervention. Where necessary, we assist with referrals to clinics, social workers, healthcare providers, and other relevant services.

Social Support & Outreach

We help reduce isolation and strengthen community connections through regular welfare checks, community outreach, support groups, and social activities. Our outreach work creates opportunities for older persons and persons with disabilities to connect with others, participate in community life, share experiences, and access information and support in a safe and welcoming environment.

Advocacy & Referrals

Itlhokomeleng assists individuals and families in navigating available government and community services. We provide information and support with referrals to clinics, social workers, government departments, disability and older-person services, and relevant social grants. We also advocate for the dignity, rights, safety, and inclusion of older persons and persons with disabilities, helping ensure that their needs and concerns are heard and addressed.

How to Access Support

  1. Contact us via phone, email, or the form on the Contact page.
  2. Share basic details (name, location, support needed).
  3. Our team will assess and link you to appropriate services.
