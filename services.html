Our Services

Support designed to promote dignity, safety, independence and inclusion for older persons and persons with disabilities.

How We Support Our Community

Itlhokomeleng Association for the Aged and Disabled is committed to supporting older persons and persons with disabilities in the Alexandra community. Our services aim to promote dignity, independence, inclusion and improved quality of life through compassionate care and community-based support.

We recognise that every individual has different needs and circumstances. Through home-based care, social support, community outreach, advocacy and referrals, we aim to connect individuals and families with appropriate assistance and available community resources.

We understand that accessing care and support can sometimes be challenging for individuals and their families. Our approach focuses on listening to people's concerns, understanding their circumstances and helping them identify appropriate support options.

Whether you are seeking support for yourself or a family member, looking for information about community services, or interested in contributing as a volunteer or sponsor, we encourage you to explore the services below and contact us for further information.

Home-Based Care

Itlhokomeleng provides compassionate home-based support to older persons and persons with disabilities in the comfort of their own homes. Home-based support can help individuals maintain dignity, comfort and a greater sense of independence in their daily lives.

Services may include home visits, basic personal care support, daily-living assistance, wellbeing monitoring, companionship and identifying needs that may require professional intervention.

Where necessary, we assist with referrals to clinics, social workers, healthcare providers and other relevant services. Families and caregivers can contact us to enquire about available support and appropriate next steps.

Social Support and Outreach

Social connection is an important part of personal wellbeing. We aim to help reduce isolation and strengthen community connections through welfare checks, community outreach, support groups and social activities.

These activities create opportunities for older persons and persons with disabilities to connect with others, participate in community life, share experiences and access useful information in a safe and welcoming environment.

Our community-focused approach recognises the importance of belonging, mutual support and participation. Families and community members can contact us to enquire about available outreach and social support opportunities.

Advocacy and Referrals

Itlhokomeleng assists individuals and families in navigating available government and community services. Finding the right information or support can be difficult, particularly when individuals are unfamiliar with the services available to them.

We provide information and support with referrals to clinics, social workers, government departments, disability and older-person services, and relevant social grant services.

We also promote the dignity, rights, safety and inclusion of older persons and persons with disabilities. Our aim is to help ensure that people's needs and concerns are heard and that they can explore appropriate support options.

How to Access Support

If you or someone you know requires assistance, you can contact Itlhokomeleng to enquire about available services. Sharing information about your circumstances and the type of support needed can help us understand your enquiry.

  1. Contact us by telephone, email or through our Contact page.
  2. Share basic details, including your name, location and the type of support required.
  3. Our team can review your enquiry and advise on appropriate available services or referral options.

Please contact us directly to confirm service availability and any requirements before making arrangements.
